IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) - VIT Chennai Student Chapter

The IEEE Robotics and Automation Society's objectives are scientific, literary and educational in character. The Society strives for the advancement of the theory and practice of robotics and automation engineering and science and of the allied arts and sciences, and for the maintenance of high professional standards among its members, all in consonance with the Constitution and Bylaws of the IEEE and with special attention to such aims within the Field of Interest of the Society.

Mission: To provide an environment for students to grow and nurture their talents under the efficient guidance of reputed IEEE and industrial experts. To keep students updated with the latest trends and technologies and promote their research and projects worldwide.

IEEE RAS VIT Chennai is the official Robotics and Automation Society student chapter at VIT Chennai. The chapter organizes technical workshops, hackathons, guest talks, and competitions in robotics, embedded systems, IoT, machine learning, and automation for students. It works closely with IEEE Madras Young Professionals and other IEEE bodies, and collaborates with other VIT Chennai clubs like IoTHINC for joint events.
